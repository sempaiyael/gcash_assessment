# FT-Transformer Paper

**Title**: Revisiting Deep Learning Models for Tabular Data
**Authors**: Gorishniy, Y., Rubachev, I., Khrulkov, V., Babenko, A.
**Venue**: NeurIPS 2021
**arXiv**: 2106.11959

## Key Contributions

- Showed a principled Transformer adaptation (Feature Tokenizer + standard encoder) achieves best avg performance among DL tabular models
- Rigorous benchmarking under identical protocols against many prior methods
- Introduced the Feature Tokenizer: categorical via lookup, numerical via learnable scaling
- Established FT-Transformer as the baseline for subsequent tabular DL research

## Authors

Yandex Research / HSE University group. Same group later produced TabR (2024).
