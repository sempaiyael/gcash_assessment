**Title**: Tabular Deep Learning Meets Nearest Neighbors

## Key Contributions

- Augments feed-forward networks with k-NN retrieval layer
- Achieves best performance among parametric tabular DL models
- Even outperforms GBDT on some benchmarks
- Same research group as FT-Transformer
