# SVD Paper

**Title**: Factorization Meets the Neighborhood: A Multifaceted Collaborative Filtering Model
**Authors**: Koren, Y.
**Venue**: KDD 2008

## Key Contributions

- Combined neighborhood-based and factorization-based CF approaches
- Introduced SVD++ (incorporating implicit feedback)
- Showed implicit signals (browsing, viewing history) improve predictions
- Highly cited foundational work in recommender systems

## Author

Yehuda Koren, Yahoo Research / later Netflix Prize winner.
