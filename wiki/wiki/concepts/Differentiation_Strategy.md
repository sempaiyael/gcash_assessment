# Differentiation Strategy

What makes this project stand out from other candidates. Every session should work toward these.

## Our Differentiators

### 1. FT-Transformer Choice
- **What**: Novel algorithm (NeurIPS 2021) instead of standard XGBoost
- **Why it stands out**: Shows awareness of cutting-edge research, ability to implement from papers
- **Talking point**: "I chose FT-Transformer because it showed Transformers work for tabular data, and I can discuss the theoretical underpinnings from the source paper"

### 2. Interpretability Story
- **What**: Feature importance via attention masks, not just accuracy numbers
- **Why it stands out**: Most candidates report metrics; we explain WHY predictions are made
- **Talking point**: "This movie is predicted to be a hit BECAUSE of director track record + genre timing + cast popularity"

### 3. Business Framing
- **What**: Every technical finding tied to business implication
- **Why it stands out**: Shows business acumen, not just technical skill
- **Talking point**: "Here's what this means for your investment decision"

### 4. Greenlight-Time Features
- **What**: Model uses only features available before production (D002)
- **Why it stands out**: Makes the model actionable, not just academic
- **Talking point**: "This model can actually be used to make investment decisions"

### 5. Bonus Recommender (If Completed)
- **What**: Went beyond requirements
- **Why it stands out**: Shows initiative and breadth
- **Talking point**: "I also built a recommender system to show I can work across ML domains"

## How to Leverage These

### In the Presentation
- Lead with the business problem, not the technical approach
- Show interpretability visualizations (feature importance, attention masks)
- Frame results as investment recommendations
- Include "Future Work" section showing depth of thinking

### In the Code
- Clean, modular structure shows engineering maturity
- README with clear setup instructions shows professionalism
- Comments explain WHY, not just WHAT

### In Interview
- Use [[concepts/Interview_Narrative]] talking points
- Be ready to discuss tradeoffs and limitations
- Show awareness of what you'd do with more time
- Connect technical choices to business value

## What Other Candidates Likely Do

| Common Approach | Our Advantage |
|----------------|---------------|
| Use XGBoost/LightGBM | FT-Transformer (novel, citable) |
| Report accuracy only | Interpretability + business framing |
| Basic visualizations | Presentation-ready, labeled charts |
| No business context | Investment-focused narrative |
| Skip bonus | Attempted (or completed) bonus |
| Code that works | Clean, reproducible, documented |
