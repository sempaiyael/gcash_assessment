## [Deliberation] Results Review as a Team (evening)
- Computed precision-coverage analysis on test predictions (read-only) — answered "can we tune to 90% precision?":
  - NO at usable coverage. Val@90% → test precision 0.53-0.67 at ≤2% coverage; oracle-on-test needs recall ≤1% for 90% precision
  - P@K slate IS the high-precision tool: FT-T P@25=0.76, P@50=0.74 (~10x base rate)
